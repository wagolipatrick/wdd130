White Water Rafting

Our mission is to provide safe, exciting, and memorable rafting experiences while helping visitors enjoy the beauty of Uganda's rivers.

Our History

White Water Rafting was founded with a simple mission — to share the thrill and beauty of Uganda's rivers with adventurers from around the world.

What started as a small group of passionate guides has grown into a trusted team offering safe, exciting, and unforgettable rafting experiences.

Over the years, we have welcomed many guests and created lasting memories through exciting adventures on the water.
