Uganda

Uganda is a landlocked country in East Africa, located along the Equator. It is bordered by Kenya to the east, South Sudan to the north, the Democratic Republic of the Congo to the west, Rwanda to the southwest, and Tanzania to the south. Uganda's capital city is Kampala. The country is known for its beautiful landscapes, wildlife, mountains, forests, and lakes. Uganda also shares a large part of Lake Victoria with Kenya and Tanzania, and the River Nile flows through the country.
